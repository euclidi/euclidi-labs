# ACOUSTICA — approved specification Revision 2.1

Approved 2026-11-27. Scope: full acoustic measurement and simulation laboratory. Revisions 1.0 (hybrid instruments and five labs), 2.0 (modular signal architecture, extended instruments, adaptive fidelity, sixth ripple tank), and 2.1 (explicit local phone mic, remote phone-to-PC mic and computer audio) remain cumulative. No requirement is removed by a partial development build.

## Experience and source acquisition

Professional hybrid scientific instrumentation; responsive desktop and mobile, crisp native-resolution displays, tactile controls, numeric entry, accessibility and reduced motion. Modular workbench with preconfigured signal routes and optional editable graph: sources, filters, mixers, gain, delay, analyzers, transducers and sinks. Support local microphones (including phones), PC/tab/system audio via permission-based screen/audio sharing and OS loopback devices, remote phone microphone via explicit peer pairing, imported audio and reference generation. Expose source settings, actual processing sample rate, channel count, known/unknown processing, clipped/dropped samples and connection state. Browser constraints are requests, not proof of unprocessed hardware. No background capture, silent upload or automatic monitoring; close tracks and pair explicitly. WebRTC compressed/resampled input is not calibrated SPL. Future native loopback bridge when browser cannot offer system audio.

## Measurement instruments

Oscilloscope (trigger, timebase, cursors, coupling, persistence), FFT spectrum and PSD (linear/log, window corrections, peak hold, harmonics), spectrogram (time/frequency zoom and accurate timestamps), levels (RMS, peak, crest, Leq, clipping, percentile, A/C/Z), frequency counter, voice/pitch/periodicity/range/note/vibrato, formants F1–F5 and bandwidth/confidence (Burg LPC vs Praat), harmonic/noise/THD/THD+N when valid, phase/correlation/coherence and Lissajous for stereo, waveform/sweep/noise/modulation generator, filter lab, controlled-excitation transfer function/impulse response/group delay, recording and export. One timestamped signal bus. Distinguish digital dBFS, calibrated estimated dB SPL and derived/simulated data. Calibration levels: no calibration; single reference; frequency response correction; independently validated hardware chain and uncertainty. Invalidate profiles when source or known gain changes. No fabricated readings or certification.

## Six experimental laboratories

1. Cymatics: Kirchhoff–Love plates, membranes, multiple materials/geometry/supports/force location, damped modal dynamics, contact-aware granular response and nodal visualization; simulated units with explicit actuator sensitivity.
2. Resonance: tubes, open/closed boundaries, temperature, coupled cavities, Helmholtz resonators, losses/end correction and virtual receivers.
3. Propagation: quantitative 2D staggered pressure/velocity FDTD, variable media, rigid/release/absorbing boundaries, geometry editor and emitters/receivers; pressure, velocity, energy, density gradient and phase. Bandlimit by validated numerical bandwidth; CFL and convergence, physical scale. Hybrid 3D apparatus/structural rendering, not full 3D propagation. Live, precision, offline, step modes with truthful timing.
4. Vocal mechanics: adjustable articulatory source-filter/digital waveguide, glottal source, oral/nasal coupling, tongue/lip geometry, synthesized response comparison; not inferred user anatomy.
5. Ripple tank: gravity-capillary surface waves with depth, surface tension, excitation, boundaries and interference; distinguish from pressure waves.
6. Wave synthesis: compound oscillators, modulation, additive Fourier synthesis, filters and explicit delay/phase; manual modular connections, safe feedback.

## Acceptance gates

Isolated frequency ≤0.1% with suitable settings; RMS/peak ≤0.05 dB; coherent FFT amplitude ≤0.1 dB; harmonics ≤0.5 dB; Parseval ≤0.1%; clean periodic pitch 50–2000 Hz ≤0.5%; synthetic formants ≤5%; coherent stereo phase ≤1°; designated A/C reference points ≤0.2 dB; filters/resampler ≤0.2/0.1 dB; PSD integrated power ≤0.2 dB; correct energy Leq; no false pitch on silence. Tube eigenfrequencies ≤1%, plate ≤2%; wave speed ≤2% in tested bandwidth; reflection ≤2%; fringe positions ≤2 cells; absorbing reflection ≤−30 dB under defined fixture; energy drift ≤1% over defined run, grid convergence, GPU agreement ≤1%; ripple dispersion, nodal migration, transducer units/phase/amplitude, graph/feedback checks. Explicit source permissions, no audio uploads, source disconnection/recovery, no unbounded growth, 30-minute continuous capture and 60 desktop/30–60 mobile FPS on identified hardware, ≤100ms basic meter latency with analysis delay disclosed, correct recording sample counts, offline core functionality, desktop/mobile browser smoke, actual playtesting. Document actual results and failures. Publication only after repository requirements and interactive checks are met.
